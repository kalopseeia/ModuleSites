ABOUT THE EXHIBITION

Nature is not just
our subject. It is our story.

Six perspectives. One living planet.

The Art of Nature explores how artists translate natural systems into color, texture, movement, and form. The exhibition moves from quiet forest spaces to open oceans and imagined landscapes.

Every work is designed to encourage a different kind of looking: close observation, listening, movement, and reflection.
